My Andragogical/Pedagogical Philosophy

Linguistics is a Tool for Empowerement!

I see language as an active, ever-changing toolkit for human connection and identity construction. I cultivate classrooms where linguistic diversity is treated as a vital asset for further language development and deeper self-reflection. I draw heavily upon Paulo Freire’s critical pedadogy framework and Ofelia García’s translanguaging framework.

In my language classrooms, I am deeply passionate about applying evidence-based practices, creating student-cented spaces, continuously innterweaving intercultural pragmatics into my instuction, and preparing my students for interactions in their target language in situations that are relevant for them.

As an teacher, I see myself as a guide first and foremost, but also as a life-long learner who gains invaluable knowledge from interactions with my students.
