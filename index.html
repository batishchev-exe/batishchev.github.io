About Me

Hi! I am a linguist and language instructor. My research focuses heavily on sociolinguistics, discourse analysis, multilingualism, identity construction, and (digital) ethnography, and sociophonetics. I recently finished my M.A. coursework at UMass Boston; currently, I am working on my Master's thesis and applying to PhD programs in sociolinguistics. I teach English as a Second or Other Language and Russian.

I am also a visual artist, a poet, and a zinester. In my free time, I like going down Wikipedia rabbit holes, practicing guitar, resting with my cats, and journaling.

I currently live, work, and study on the unceded ancestral lands of the Dena'ina Athabaskan people.
