About Me

Hi! I am a sociolinguist and language educator in Anchorage, Alaska. I recently completed my M.A. coursework in Applied Linguistics at UMass Boston. I am writing my Master's thesis on diaspora sociophonetics and sociopolitical stance and applying to doctoral programs[cite: 17, 25].

My research focuses on how minoritized and marginalized speakers navigate grammatical systems and identity construction in discourse[cite: 17, 19]. In addition to my scholarship, I teach adult ESOL and Russian[cite: 17, 24].

I live, work, and study on the unceded ancestral lands of the Dena'ina Athabaskan people.
