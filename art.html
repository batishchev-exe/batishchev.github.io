Artist Statement

I am a Moscow-born multidisciplinary artist, poet, and sociolinguist currently based on the unceded Dena'ina lands in Anchorage, Alaska. An autodidact by nature, I am self-taught – but is anyone really fully self-taught? – in a variety of mediums including, but not limited to printmaking, photography, zines, painting, drawing, performance, and, of course, mixed media. With my art, I like to explore themes of identity and becoming, intersection of the meraphysical with the material, nostalgia and belonging. Lately, all my commission projects were digital art pieces; I also enjoy working with analog mediums, especially when it comes to manipulation of the 2D space via collage, 3D elements, and using unconvential art products, such as office supplies.

My art and my research are driven by one and the same: who are we – humans? What are the deepest desires of our hearts? How do we tell the world about who we are?

Feel free to check out my visual works here and my poetry on Substack! For other written work, please check out my blog.

Poetry

I publish select poetry on my Substack. I have been writing poetry since a very young age. In my 20s, I dedicated a substancial amount of time to building up my local poetry community by submitting to local publications, participating in writing groups, and hosting a local poetry slam (2023-2026). My poetry appears in Year Round Queer, Butch-Femme Press, and Alaska Women Speak. My poem ice migration was nominated for the Pushcart Prize 2023.
