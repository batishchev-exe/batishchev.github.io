Current & Recent Projects

The LGBT Acronym as Macro-Political and Micro-Interpersonal Synecdoche

LSA 2027 Oral Presentation

Co-authored with Liza Sulkin • Corpus-Assisted Discourse Studies (CADS) / (Auto)Ethnography

Investigates a 1M-token corpus of private peer interactions to map how a Russian-speaking youth enclave 1) repurposed the state-weaponized acronym ЛГБТ, 2) navigated the boundary of slur reclamation and usage, and 3) took stances corresponding to specific queer identity labels during the 2010s post-"propaganda" law era.

Do Men Deserve Flowers? (...) Envy, and Desire in Contemporary Online Russian Discourse

LSA 2027 Oral Presentation

Single-Authored • Russian Digital Discourse / CADS

A study of 27,300 comments analyzing ideological policing of masculinity around queer-coded social media trends, examining how socio-economic roles (e.g., the indexicality of мужик) displace biological essentialism in vernacular gender discourse.

Pragmatics and Asymmetric Essentialization of Theyfab

LSA 2027 Oral Presentation

Co-authored with Sumeyye Nabieva • Trans Linguistics / Pragmatics in Discourse

Examines the corpus-based trajectory of the neologism theyfab across online communities, analyzing how birth-assigned medical categories are grammaticalized into intra-community boundary policing and pejorative gatekeeping.

Vowel Reduction & Identity Indexicality in Diaspora Surzhyk

Master's Thesis (In Progress)

University of Massachusetts Boston • Sociophonetics & Language Contact

An mixed-methods sociophonetic investigation into whether Ukrainian diaspora speakers retain non-reduced vowel realizations when speaking Russian varieties, testing how vowel (non)reduction indexes post-2022 sociopolitical stance and regional identification.
